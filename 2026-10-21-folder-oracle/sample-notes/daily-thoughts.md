# Daily Thoughts

## On productivity

Productivity is not about doing more things, it is about finishing the right ones. My best days start with one clear goal written down before I open email or messages. Small daily wins compound: one tiny project a day is about 250 projects a year.

## Routines that help

- Morning: water, ten minutes of walking, then the hardest task first while my energy is high.
- Work in focused blocks of 50 minutes with a short break. Phone in another room.
- Keep a "parking lot" note for distracting ideas so they do not hijack the current block.
- End the day by writing tomorrow's first task. Starting is easier when the decision is already made.

## When motivation is low

Lower the bar instead of skipping. Five minutes of work still keeps the streak alive, and usually five minutes turns into thirty. Consistency beats intensity.

## Rest

Rest is part of productivity, not the opposite of it. Sleep, long walks and time with friends are where a lot of good ideas quietly show up. Burnout costs far more time than a slow afternoon.

## Reminders to self

- Done is better than perfect, especially for daily builds.
- Ship small, then improve.
- Notice what drains energy and do less of it.
- Celebrate progress, even tiny progress.
